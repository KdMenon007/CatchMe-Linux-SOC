## Asset Guidelines

- Keep finalized assets inside this directory.
- Do not store credentials, private keys, passwords, tokens, or other secrets.
- Avoid placing raw sensitive evidence in the assets directory.
- Keep evidence and screenshots in their designated project directories.
- Use descriptive and consistent filenames.
- Preserve the finalized banner after project completion to maintain repository consistency.
